Terms of use

RIKI-LIFE-GENERAL is an owner-operated personal organization tool maintained by Riki Yanai. These terms describe its intended use; there is no public registration, paid subscription or public access to the operator's workspace.

Authorized use

The app is for accounts and information the operator owns or is authorized to access. Google permissions do not authorize access to someone else's account or override another service's rules. Actions such as sending correspondence or modifying records require the operator's instruction.

Review and availability

Saved snapshots and assistant-generated summaries may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Review the original source before relying on important information, and review consequential actions before carrying them out. The tool is maintained for personal use without a commitment to uninterrupted availability or support.

Privacy and ending access

The privacy policy explains data access, processing, storage and retention. The account holder can revoke Google authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation does not itself erase saved copies or backups.
