Privacy policy

RIKI-LIFE-GENERAL is a personal information-management tool operated by Riki Yanai for the operator's own authorized accounts. It is not offered as a public multi-user service.

Google information accessed

With consent, the app can access Gmail messages, attachments and mailbox metadata; Google Drive file contents and metadata; and Google Calendar calendars, events and reminders. Its granted permissions also allow Gmail drafting/sending, Drive file management and Calendar event management. Such actions are performed at the operator's direction.

How information is used

Information is used for the operator's personal reference, search, organization, planning, correspondence and requested actions. Saved snapshots provide continuity and offline access. The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage, processing and sharing

Saved information may reside on operator-controlled devices and in a private GitHub repository used for backups and history. Operator-authorized assistant services and connected tools may process information when needed for a requested task. These service providers are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. These public information pages do not expose those private records.

Google user data must not be used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Any assistant service used with this data must be configured and used consistently with that restriction. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security and access

OAuth tokens are used to access Google APIs without placing the account password in the app. The local OAuth helper stores its credential file with owner-only file permissions. Personal snapshots are intended for private storage, not publication on this website.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The operator controls how long saved records are retained; the app does not apply a universal automatic-deletion period. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete existing local copies, private repository history or backups. Those copies must be removed separately; older versions may remain until the relevant history and backups are also removed.

Contact and changes

For questions, corrections or data-management requests, contact riki@yuuz.co. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
